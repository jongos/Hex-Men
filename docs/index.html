Training Scenario

Enter the Danger Room

A requester submits work. An approver approves it. The interface says “Success.” The requester still has no idea what happened. Here’s how the team could tackle it.

  1. Spiral rehearses the handoff.

    What can each role see? Who owns the next step? What happens if the approval arrives late or the notification fails?

  2. Dazzler gives the answer a place on screen.

    Shape a clear status, useful hierarchy and an obvious next action.

  3. Style Science brings the checklist.

    Declare what must be visible and readable, then check the available observations. Missing evidence stays unknown.

  4. Longshot runs the journey.

    Submit, leave, return and look for the approved result. Record what happened and how to verify a fix.

  5. Shatterstar challenges the extra screens.

    Could one destination replace three? Check that retrieval, comparison, export and recovery still work.

  6. Mojo writes the line people actually need.

    “Approved. Your final version is ready.”

Meanwhile, Back at Headquarters

Colossus inventories selected Windows development folders and surfaces questions worth investigating. Audits remain read-only. Restricted cleanup requires fresh user approval of an exact-file plan. Even the heavy hitter knows when to keep its hands off.

Choose Your First Mission

Hex Men is a directory of independent projects. Start with one focused job, or carry its findings into the next.

“Make this look like our product.”Dazzler

“Can someone actually finish this task?”Longshot

“How do we check this design decision?”Style Science

“Where does this workflow lose people?”Spiral

“What can we simplify without breaking the job?”Shatterstar

“Can we just say what we mean?”Mojo

“What’s in this workspace, and what needs investigation?”Colossus

Dazzler, Longshot, Spiral, Shatterstar and Mojo are agent skills. Style Science provides JavaScript and Python tools. Colossus pairs a Rust CLI with a Codex/Claude skill; its Rust CLI supports Windows, with restricted user-approved exact-file deletion on local NTFS. Follow each project’s setup instructions.

Powers With Receipts

Inspect the Evidence

Longshot reports agent-observed behavior, not human research. Style Science’s GDC is an early engineering prototype for checking declared requirements, not a validated score for design quality. Spiral and Shatterstar provide research-informed methods; their effectiveness has not been validated in human studies.

Colossus reports an inventory, not permission to delete. The Rust CLI does not support Linux or macOS scanning. Its separate Python metadata survey is read-only; native macOS/Linux validation remains limited. Cleanup is permanent, restricted to supported regular files on local Windows NTFS, and requires fresh approval for each exact plan. No redistribution license has been selected for its public source.
