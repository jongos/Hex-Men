# Hex Men

**Extraordinary powers. One mission: better products.**

Every product has an origin story. Too many end with twelve tabs, a mystery button and “We’re delighted to inform you that your request may have been successfully submitted.”

**Time to assemble the Hex Men!**

Hex(idecimal) Men brings together seven independent skills and tools for AI agents: design with character, inspectable design decisions, UI/UX testing, workflow rehearsal, product simplification, sharper writing and developer-workspace audits.

Your villains: confusing interfaces, dropped handoffs, feature bloat, workspace mysteries and copy that takes three paragraphs to say “Done.”

## Enter the Danger Room

**Training scenario: an approval flow with a disappearing ending.**

The Hex suite is largely for  working on design projects: app development, document creation, front-end engineering, UI designs, and the related copy. Each of these skills are designed to help make agent work feel more human. Each skill takes an approach based on design principles and take a computational approach to design instead of being trained 'copying' existing websites.  

Here’s how the team could tackle it:

1. **Spiral rehearses the handoff.** What can each role see? Who owns the next step? What happens if the approval arrives late or the notification fails?
2. **Dazzler gives the answer a place on screen.** Shape a clear status, useful hierarchy and an obvious next action.
3. **Style Science brings the checklist.** Declare what must be visible and readable, then check the available observations. Missing evidence stays unknown.
4. **Longshot runs the journey.** Submit, leave, return and look for the approved result. Record what happened and how to verify a fix.
5. **Shatterstar challenges the extra screens.** Could one destination replace three? Check that retrieval, comparison, export and recovery still work.
6. **Mojo writes the line people actually need.** “Approved. Your final version is ready.”

**Meanwhile, back at headquarters:** Colossus inventories the selected Windows development folders and surfaces questions worth investigating. Audit findings never authorize deletion. Restricted cleanup requires explicit approval of an exact-file plan. Even the heavy hitter knows when to keep its hands off.

That’s a team-up you direct. Hex Men is a directory of independent projects; choose one for a focused job or carry its findings into the next.
